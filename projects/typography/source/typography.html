The art of clean typography in web design

Why readable text is the foundation of every great blog

Published ● july 2026 ● 6 min read

typography is more than choosing a beautiful font .It defines how users experience content , how long they stay, and how easily they understand your message.

Why typography matters

poor typography can ruin even the best-written content.proper line-height, letter-spacing, and aligment improve readability and reduce eye strain.

"typography is the craft of endowing human language with a durable visual form "

Key typography rules

  • Use unitless line-height for scalability
  • Avoid large letter-spacing in body text
  • Don't justify text without hyphenation
